## Decisions

### Michigan

The FY2026 budget permits LARA to quote customized nonconfidential listings, but no current bulk product, machine schema, recurring delivery contract, or API is published. The MiBusiness portal is interactive and its website terms prohibit automated and commercial reuse without separate written permission. A nine-digit Division identifier is only a candidate key; the new platform's identifier lifecycle is undocumented. Registered offices can be resident-agent locations, and legal standing does not prove operation.

### Tennessee

The Secretary of State advertises purchase and download of its Business Entity Database through TNCaB, but publishes no current price, exact schema, anonymous transport, cadence, or change contract. The SOS control number is a candidate key without a public lifecycle guarantee. Official Revenue guidance confirms that an inactive or administratively dissolved entity may still do business, so registry status cannot become an operating-status assertion.

### Massachusetts

950 CMR 113.15 authorizes a paid CIMS full extract on a monthly basis in weekly increments, but the filing office supplies its data elements and layout only on request. The wording does not establish baseline-versus-delta mechanics, deletions, corrections, replay, checksums, or automation. Public search identifiers and address roles are not a bulk contract, website scraping is prohibited, and downstream commercial publication rights are unpublished.

### Arizona

The Corporation Commission offers a paid full quote-delimited extraction by CD-ROM or download and partial extractions by CD-ROM or email after a signed request and purpose statement. Its public materials list broad field groups but no exact current header, row population, stable-ID lifecycle, status dictionary, recurring cadence, deletion semantics, checksums, or unattended transport. Commercial use is purpose-specific under Arizona law and the request form; the January 2026 platform migration adds another unresolved schema and identity boundary.
