# Mississippi Business Reports offline contract

This connector is a bounded, offline contract for an operator-supplied standard XLS or XLSX export from the official [Mississippi Business Reports](https://corp.sos.ms.gov/corpreporting/Corp/BusinessSearch3). The published report documents business name and ID, other names, profile and domicile type, registry status, up to three NAICS codes, principal address fields, postal code, and formation date. Its Excel export is limited to 300,000 results; a complete export requires separate contact with Business Services. This connector performs neither action.

A package beneath `data/imports/mississippi-business-report/packages/<package-id>/` must contain exactly `selection.json`, one `original.xls` or `original.xlsx`, and `records.jsonl`. The JSONL is an operator-derived projection with the exact published field roster encoded by the connector. Original and derived bytes, sizes, row count, export query description, and transformation declaration are hash-bound. The filename and declared format must agree, but the connector deliberately does not parse workbook internals or infer authenticity from an extension. Workbook parsing and reproducible equivalence are not claimed, so records remain `source_native: false`, `source_authenticity_verified: false`, `reproducible_extraction_verified: false`, and `admission_eligible: false`.

Business ID is retained as the publisher-described six- or seven-digit candidate identifier. Registry status is preserved verbatim but is not proof of current operation. Principal address is explicitly administrative registration evidence, not a verified physical site, and may be residential. ZIP5 and ZIP4 are separate fields. The connector rejects extra columns, malformed values, duplicate IDs, changed inputs, packages outside the fixed import root, more than 300,000 rows, and any selection that asserts acquisition, completeness, redistribution, admission, enrollment, network use, or pointer mutation.

`statewide_complete` is always false. A result containing exactly 300,000 rows carries an explicit ceiling/truncation warning, but any smaller result is still partial because no governed full-snapshot receipt exists. The connector intentionally contains no mechanism to escalate completeness. Every record carries `export_policy: local-review-only`, `production_enrollment: false`, and `current_pointer_written: false`. It returns only an in-memory, person-free projection; it writes no release or pointer and is not integrated into managed operations or the national registry.

Rights, retention, transformation, geocoding, derived publication, redistribution, status vocabulary, address role, refresh cadence, and full-export controls remain unresolved. The source policy therefore prohibits redistribution and limits all inputs and outputs to local review. No browser, API, HTTP request, source acquisition, publisher contact, admission, or production change is implemented.
