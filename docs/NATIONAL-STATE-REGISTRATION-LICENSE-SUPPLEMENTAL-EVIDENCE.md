# National state registration/license supplemental evidence

`national-state-registration-license-supplemental-evidence@1.0.0` is an immutable, pointer-free, local-review aggregate built only from retained bytes. It adds source-relative administrative address evidence from CT, DE, CO, OR, IA, NY, FL, PA, and WA without changing the lifecycle base.

The release contains a dense 48,194-key governed ZIP cohort, including explicit zero rows, 60 governed geography-disposition scopes, and nine separate issuer-state marginals. A ZIP not in the governed cohort is an outside-cohort lookup state; it is not rewritten into a state or ZIP by prefix. ZIP+4 remains a separate null field in this aggregate.

Ten dimensions remain separate. Oregon legal registrations and assumed-business-name brands are not merged. The five evidence classes are organization-registration-address, business-license-address, legal-registration-address, assumed-business-name-address, and construction-contractor-license. Washington evidence is a contractor-license mailing-address cohort, not premises or current-operation proof.

Issuer-state marginals conserve eligible plus missing/ineligible source-address rows. They are comparison-only and do not overwrite governed address-geography disposition. Counts are source-address memberships, not unique businesses, verified current operations, physical sites, completeness percentages, or cross-source additive business totals.

The manifest pins all nine native source manifests and policies, the retained eight-source broad aggregate, the WA aggregate artifact, the governed ZIP-disposition manifest/artifact, and retained refresh assessments. Newer aggregate editions were observed but not collected for DE, IA, NY, and WA; those observations are refresh signals only and are not net organization creation, closure, operation, or completeness. The build performs no acquisition, network request, pointer write, or production enrollment.
