# National FMCSA registrant principal-office coverage

This retained-only governed aggregate replays the exact pinned FMCSA Company Census selected snapshot and the pinned Census geography release. It makes no network requests and is not enrolled in production or generic business totals.

The release contains one national summary, 56 jurisdiction rows, and the retained 39,151-row ZIP union. Counts describe accepted source-designated active registrations at reported principal-office addresses. They do not establish unique businesses, current operation outside FMCSA's definition, public storefronts, address deliverability, or nationwide business completeness.

Carrier operation is preserved as an exact partition. Entity roles and source classes are explicitly nonexclusive; their totals can exceed record counts. Hazardous-materials values are retained as source classifications. Coordinates are not retained. ZIP5 and ZIP4 remain separate.

Source classes use the documented FMCSA primary class/government vocabulary. Free-text `OTHER-*` source values are collapsed into the single governed `OTHER` bucket so identifier-like or sensitive free text cannot escape through an aggregate label.

Only aggregate counts are published. Names, addresses, USDOT/docket numbers, and record identifiers are forbidden because a registrant can be an individual proprietor and a principal office can be home-based.
