## Georgia: promising export, current delivery contract unresolved

DECAL's [Provider Data Export](https://families.decal.ga.gov/Provider/Data) is an official candidate. Search-index material advertised downloads and separate licensed Child Care Learning Centers, family-home and nonlicensed program options. The direct page request returned HTTP 404 during inspection. Indexed content is not proof that a working current export endpoint was accessed; no retry or export action followed.

The official [data dictionary](https://www.decal.ga.gov/documents/attachments/dataexportdatadictionary.pdf), dated July 1, 2020, describes `Provider_Number` (also the license number for licensed programs), `Location` (name), premises `Address`, `City`, `State`, `Zip`, `County`, `LicenseCapacity` and `Provider_Type`. `CCLC` denotes Child Care Learning Centers. Mailing addresses, email and phone must not substitute for premises fields. No latitude/longitude or distinct ZIP4 field is documented in that dictionary. Retain nullable coordinates and separately represented ZIP fields; do not infer their availability from the existence of a map.

Indexed [legal notice](https://families.decal.ga.gov/legal-notice) material dated May 2026 includes use-as-assent, anti-overburdening and intended-access restrictions, warranty disclaimers, and a hold-harmless provision. This is not a completed source-use review or an established redistribution license. Preserve existing Georgia Secretary of State holds; this candidate is a different publisher and industry scope.

Before a row connector is enrolled, establish the actual current download URL/method/format, schema, count and update semantics through ordinary authorized access, and review the applicable complete publisher notice. A historical dictionary and indexed interface labels alone are insufficient to build the dependent acquisition process.
