# Florida and Oregon industry-source triage — September 8, 2026

These are source-contract gaps, not claims that the states have no businesses. Existing broad state registry data remains separate and unchanged. No facility rows were acquired, records request submitted, payment made or app schedule enabled during this triage.

## Florida food-service licenses

The official [Hotels and Restaurants public-records page](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/hotels-restaurants/public-records/) and [lodging records page](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/hotels-restaurants/lodging-public-records/) returned HTTP 403 during direct inspection. No access-control workaround was attempted.

An accessible [official license-download guide](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/sto/documents/readme.pdf) describes delimited public records and a historical schema. Indexed official listing material describes later risk-level columns absent from that schema. Consequently neither a current download URL nor a compatible current food-service file contract was established. A parser built from the old layout would risk shifting fields.

Next: obtain the currently published direct file/schema contract through ordinary authorized access and validate it together before building acquisition. Keep permanent food-service premises distinct from mobile, catering, vending and temporary-event license classes. Mailing addresses and license-holder contacts must not be mistaken for physical premises.

## Oregon food and childcare

The [ODA food licensing overview](https://www.oregon.gov/oda/food-safety/fs-licensing/Pages/default.aspx) covers food retail and other regulated activities; restaurant and mobile-food responsibilities also involve counties. Public license search controls are not evidence of a supported bulk API or export schema.

The [ODA public-records process](https://www.oregon.gov/oda/about-us/Pages/public-records.aspx) can involve written requests and fees. No request or financial commitment was made. A useful future extract needs premises identifiers, license types/status, facility addresses, separately represented ZIP extensions, update semantics and documented usage terms. Domestic kitchens need an explicit exclusion or distinct scope.

The [DELC data page](https://www.oregon.gov/delc/data/pages/default.aspx) and [childcare search](https://www.oregon.gov/delc/families/Pages/find-childcare.aspx) establish public information surfaces, but no record-level bulk delivery contract was verified. The [DELC records process](https://www.oregon.gov/delc/Pages/public-records.aspx) distinguishes sensitive home-provider information. Do not infer unrestricted extraction or redistribution from dashboard visibility.

Next: establish an official business-premises-only delivery contract before connector enrollment. Meanwhile, pursue other independently accessible state/industry sources rather than occupying an agent with retries against these unresolved surfaces.
