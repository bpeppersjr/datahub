Observed September 8, 2026. A peer researched NC/VA documentation in parallel with the integrator's Minnesota construction assessment. The integrator independently checked the official pages below. This is source-discovery evidence, not app enrollment or acquired business coverage. Existing broad-registry Queue 5 holds remain unchanged.

## Virginia: delivery documented, use restrictions require resolution

The [VQB5 download page](https://earlychildhoodquality.doe.virginia.gov/download-data/) links a 2024–2025 quality/location workbook and a 2025–2026 participation workbook. The former includes administrator-reported site information and separately verified ratings; the latter reflects program eligibility and annual measurement requirements. Neither is described as a real-time census of active licensed businesses. These are distinct observation periods and must not be merged into one current-status assertion. Workbooks were not requested or inspected.

The peer could not retrieve the linked web policy, but the integrator's subsequent request succeeded: `https://www.childcare.virginia.gov/?navid=225` resolves to [VDOE Web Policies](https://www.doe.virginia.gov/about-vdoe/web-policies). The disclaimer permits non-commercial downloads with notices retained and requires written VDOE permission for commercial use. The copyright section adds a permission statement. Do not treat public download links or internal storage as proof that the intended data-hub use qualifies. Resolve the intended use and applicable permission before acquisition; this assessment grants none.

Next work, if that gate is resolved: inspect bounded workbook metadata/schema, distinguish center/site types from family homes and personal contacts, identify stable keys, and preserve program year separately from license and operational status. No source count, geocode availability, household exclusion, license verification, commercial rights or public export is established.

## North Carolina: dashboard is not the current record feed

The official [Data Behind the Dashboard](https://ncchildcare.ncdhhs.gov/Home/Data/Dashboards/Data-Behind-the-Dashboard) page reports maintenance and directs requests for 2022-current data to the DCDEE data-request portal. It describes county-oriented dashboard outputs and underlying regulatory observations that may lag site changes. That is not a verified current, row-level acquisition contract.

Do not implement a current-business connector by assuming a functioning historical dashboard is a live facility feed. Next locate a documented current public delivery contract or prepare a schema-only publisher inquiry for operator review. No portal request, email, account, row enumeration or dataset download was submitted. National-source NC evidence is separate from direct NC-publisher access.
