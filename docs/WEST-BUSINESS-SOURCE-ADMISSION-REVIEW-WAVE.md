# Western business-source admission-review wave

`west-business-source-admission-review-wave@1.0.0` composes the immutable October 3, 2026 Arizona, California, Nevada and New Mexico broad-organization source reassessments into one deterministic review unit. It independently binds the promoted national-status federation manifest and `gap-ledger.json` by SHA-256, and it accepts a state only while that federation still classifies it as a current `missing-retained-broad-organization-layer` / `admission-review` gap with acquisition authority false.

This wave performs no network request, source action, acquisition, payment, contact, account creation, terms acceptance, automation, pointer change or production admission. All four decisions remain `HOLD`; their documented official-source routes and next actions are review evidence, not permission to use them. The wave adds no business records, broad-business coverage or active-business/completeness claim.

The wave is loaded with `loadWestBusinessSourceAdmissionReviewWave()` from `runner/west-business-source-admission-review-wave.mjs`. Its `wave_id` is deterministic over the bound lineage, scope, controls, claims and state review rows. A changed assessment must use a successor assessment identity; a changed federation or gap ledger must be explicitly rebound rather than silently accepted.
