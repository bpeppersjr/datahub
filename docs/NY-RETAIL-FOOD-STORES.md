# New York Retail Food Stores

This source publishes the official New York State Department of Agriculture and Markets annual snapshot of licensed retail food stores. It materially improves physical-location coverage for grocery stores, supermarkets, convenience stores, bodegas, and other Department-licensed retail food establishments across New York.

## Source and authority

- Dataset: `Retail Food Stores` (`9a8c-vfzj`)
- Publisher: New York State Department of Agriculture and Markets, Division of Food Safety & Inspection
- Access: anonymous read-only OPEN-NY metadata and SODA API
- Posting frequency: annual
- Time period: current snapshot
- Terms: OPEN-NY Terms of Use; the catalog specifies no dataset-specific license

The connector pins the official publisher, publication state, scope metadata, 15 selected source fields, field types, and source refresh timestamp. It excludes the platform-generated computed-region column before acquisition. It uses stable license-number keyset pagination, compares total and distinct-license counts, and rechecks source metadata and counts before publication.

## Entity and location semantics

Each unique six-digit license number creates one provisional organization candidate. That identity means “the organization represented by this license record”; it does not merge repeated entity names or assert a legal parent company, chain, network, or cross-source match.

A row creates a provisional physical site and establishment only when its source-reported physical address contains a street number, street name, city, New York state code, and valid five-digit ZIP. A row missing only a street number can still contribute source-reported physical-location evidence to aggregate ZIP coverage, but it does not create a site.

The `georeference` field is generated by the Open Data platform from address components. It is retained only when numeric and within broad New York bounds, labeled as an address-component centroid, and never represented as an independently verified premise coordinate. Multiple stores can share a coordinate.

## Establishment codes

The attached Department code reference is encoded as source definitions. The current snapshot also contains code `Y`, which is absent from that reference. The connector preserves `Y` as undocumented and does not guess its meaning. Every accepted row must retain source operation type `Store` and include establishment code `A`; changes are quarantined as source-filter drift.

## Privacy and distribution

Entity names can identify natural persons, and licensed physical addresses can be residences. Therefore:

- selected source snapshots are internal;
- normalized record-level artifacts are local-review-only;
- aggregate ZIP and source summaries may be redistributed under OPEN-NY terms with attribution, refresh timestamp, provenance, and limitations.

## Limitations

- The source is an annual snapshot and may not reflect day-to-day operations, current hours, public access, or continuous operation.
- It is not every grocery store, food seller, New York business, or United States business.
- Source square footage, county labels, names, addresses, and geocodes are not independently verified.
- ZIP syntax and Census ZCTA context do not establish current USPS deliverability. USPS operational validity remains unverified until an authorized denominator is integrated.
