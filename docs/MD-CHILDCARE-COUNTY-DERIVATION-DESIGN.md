1. Preserve `md-childcare-centers-internal@1.0.0` unchanged. Its county/ZCTA prohibition is an internal processing boundary, not a newly discovered publisher ban. Pin a new derived policy for internal point-to-Census-county relationships only, with State of Maryland, MSDE Office of Child Care, Maryland EXCELS and Census attribution. 2. Preserve `retained-childcare-county-relations@1.0.0` replay. Its inspector reconstructs the original loader output, so widening that loader in place would invalidate prior evidence. Use a separate successor module or explicit version dispatch that retains the historical implementation.
3. Bind the existing registry, county geography, all original source relationships, the new policy and complete Maryland metadata. Verify inputs before and after derivation, with cancellation and immutable publication. Perform zero source requests.
4. Reconcile all 12,206 selected candidates. Only the 1,772 Maryland eligibility decisions may change; Pennsylvania assignments, other dispositions, candidate IDs, ZIP5/ZIP4 and original source evidence must remain unchanged. 5. Replace the PA-specific reported-state comparison in the successor with a canonical state/FIPS lookup. 6. Keep identity matching, physical-site/current-operation verification, public export and national promotion disabled. Enrollment of a successfully verified successor into the map is an explicit later step, not a side effect of building it.

Require old-manifest replay; exact-policy gating; changed/missing notice or metadata rejection; Maryland and cross-state synthetic points; boundary ambiguity, invalid/missing points and unknown CRS; exact disposition conservation; byte-identical original source and postal fields; required attribution; independent offline successor replay; and cancellation, ownership-safe staging and drift tests.
